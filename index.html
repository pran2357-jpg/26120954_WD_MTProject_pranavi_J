INNOVATION · ENTREPRENEURSHIP

QwikQ

What if waiting in a queue didn't have to be part of the experience?

The Observation

Long queues and unpredictable crowd congestion can turn ordinary experiences into frustrating ones. I started exploring how this everyday problem could be approached differently.

The Idea

QwikQ is a crowd-management concept that combines real-time crowd density with historical data to help anticipate congestion and improve crowd flow.

What I Explored

How technology, predictive insights and thoughtful problem-solving could make managing crowded spaces more efficient.

"Sometimes, the best ideas begin with noticing what everyone else has learned to tolerate."
